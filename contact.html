About the developer

Nate Moore

MLIS-trained digital preservationist and developer working at the intersection of GLAM, digital humanities, and the open web. Whatiiif is a personal project focused on the access layer of IIIF, with a mission of making it easier for researchers, educators, and curious readers to point precisely at things inside digitized primary sources, regardless of which institution hosts them.

If you're at an institution and interested in supporting the project's continued development, please get in touch.
